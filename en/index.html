Privacy Policy

How GroupAlarm handles alarms, holidays, and purchase information.

GroupAlarm does not require an account. Alarm plans are primarily stored on your device. The app contacts relevant services for public holiday data and Apple in-app purchases.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy applies to the GroupAlarm iPhone app and this policy page. “We” refers to the developer of GroupAlarm.

1. Data on your device

Alarms, group names, times, repeat rules, custom holidays, selected regions, and app preferences are stored in the app’s data area on your device. GroupAlarm uses Apple AlarmKit to schedule and manage system alarms on your device. We do not offer accounts or cloud sync, and we do not upload your alarm names or plans to our servers.

2. Public holiday requests

When you select a public holiday region or refresh holiday data, the app may request available regions or holidays for a selected region and year from Nager.Date (nagerholidays.com). It may retrieve mainland China holiday data from GitHub’s raw.githubusercontent.com. Some yearly data is bundled with the app and needs no download. These requests reveal the region and year in the request path and may expose network information such as your IP address to the data provider. The app does not include your alarm content in these requests. Each provider’s own policy governs its handling of connection information.

3. Purchases and system services

Trials, one-time purchases, and purchase restoration are handled by Apple StoreKit and the App Store. The app reads Apple-verified product, transaction, and entitlement information to determine available features. We do not receive payment card details. Apple may process purchase information under its own policies. iOS manages alarm permission and scheduling.

4. Feedback, analytics, and sharing

If you choose “Report a bug” in the app, it opens your mail app with a prefilled subject. Only content you choose to send reaches us. Information you provide through the contact option below is used to respond to and handle your request. The app has no third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app tracking.

5. Retention, deletion, and choices

Alarm and holiday data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, use “Reset all plans,” or uninstall the app. A reset also attempts to cancel related system alarms; if cancellation temporarily fails, the app retries or prompts you to take action. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies. You can manage alarm permission in iOS Settings.

6. This page and changes

This static page is hosted by GitHub Pages. We have not added our own cookies, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels. GitHub may process information required to serve the page under its policies. If the app’s data practices materially change, we will update this page and its effective date.

7. Contact

For privacy or data deletion questions, contact us through GitHub Issues. Please do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
